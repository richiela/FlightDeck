Harper Wins

• Harper sets three darts (S/D/T and bulls count). Everyone else must hit the same bed in the same order.

• If Harper misses the board: that slot is FREE (always greens) and Harper loses 1 point per miss.

• 3 hits: Harper −2. 2 hits: Harper −1. 1: push. 0: you −1 and Harper +1.

• Everyone starts at 5 points. Hit 0 and you’re out. Knock Harper to 0 and the players win!
